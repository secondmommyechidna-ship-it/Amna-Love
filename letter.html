A Letter From My Heart To Amna

Words that always seem to find their way back to you.

I never knew that a single person could turn ordinary days into something so bright. Your kindness, your quiet strength, and the way you listen make me feel like I belong in a story that was written for the two of us.

When I look at you, I feel calm and excited all at once. You are the voice I search for in a crowded room and the warmth I reach for when the world goes quiet.

Thank you for being you — for the patience, the laughter, and the little moments that feel like forever. I hope you keep this letter close and feel every word the way I feel every heartbeat that spells your name.
